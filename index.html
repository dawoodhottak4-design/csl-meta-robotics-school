Age-Structured Robotics Curriculum

Progressive learning roadmap designed to evolve alongside student cognitive growth.

3-5

Robotics Discovery

Screen-free, play-based introduction to logic, sequencing, patterns, and motor skills.

6-8

Junior Robotics

Transitioning to block coding, motorized models, touch/tilt sensors, and basic mechanics.

9-10

Robotics Builder

Multi-sensor integration, variables, structural engineering, and collaborative team builds.

11-13

AI Innovator

Introduction to Python, Machine Learning workflows, autonomous navigation, and data analysis.

14-16

Advanced Robotics & AI

Advanced Python & C++, Computer Vision algorithms, national/international robotics competition prep, and capstone engineering projects.
